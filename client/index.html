Meet the Riverwalk Dental Group Team

Our practice is built around a simple idea: you deserve a dental team that truly listens. Led by founder Dr. Payal Chokshi, our doctors and staff take the time to understand your needs, explain your options, and make sure you feel at ease. Every provider is committed to treating you like family.

Our Doctors Bring Specialist-Level Care to Every Visit

Most general dental practices refer out for complex care. Not Riverwalk. We have a periodontist and an oral surgeon right here on our team. That means you get advanced treatment without the hassle of traveling to another office.

Missing teeth can affect how you eat, speak, and feel about yourself. Dental implants are the closest thing to natural teeth, and at Riverwalk Dental Group, we handle the entire process in-house.
