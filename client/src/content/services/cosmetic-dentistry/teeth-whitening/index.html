01. Why Choose Professional Teeth Whitening?

Over-the-counter whitening products can be tempting, but they often fall short of your expectations. Professional teeth whitening at our practice offers key advantages:

  • •Strength: We use professional-grade bleaching agents that lift deep stains far more effectively than drugstore strips or trays.
  • •Speed: Most patients achieve multiple shades of improvement in a single, hour long visit.
  • •Safety: Your treatment is supervised by a dentist who protects your gums and ensures the gel is applied correctly.
  • •Personalization: We match the whitening shade to your natural tooth color and your desired brightness for a result that looks natural, not overdone.

Our team will discuss your goals and recommend the approach that fits your lifestyle, whether you want dramatic improvement quickly or a gradual touch-up at home.

02. What to Expect During Your Whitening Visit

Your whitening appointment starts with a consultation and a careful shade assessment. We clean your teeth, apply a protective barrier to your gums, then apply the whitening gel. A special light may be used to accelerate the process. After the gel sits for the prescribed time, it is removed and your new shade is checked. The entire visit takes about 60 to 90 minutes, and you walk out with a noticeably brighter smile.

Throughout the process, we check in on your comfort. If you have any sensitivity, we can adjust the treatment to keep you at ease.

If you want the quickest, most dramatic change, in-office whitening is the way to go. If you prefer to whiten gradually at home, we can provide professional take-home trays with gel customized for your teeth.

03. Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

Teeth whitening works best for surface stains from coffee, tea, wine, and tobacco. Deeper discoloration from medication or injury may need a different approach, such as veneers or bonding. During your consultation, we will examine your teeth and discuss your stain type to make sure whitening is a good fit.

Patients with sensitive teeth can still whiten safely. We use desensitizing agents and tailor the gel concentration and application time to your needs. Our goal is to give you a brighter smile without unnecessary discomfort.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Teeth Whitening across Port St. Lucie, Saint Lucie West, Stuart, and Jupiter

How long do teeth whitening results last? 01 //

Results typically last six months to two years, depending on your habits. Avoiding stain-causing foods and drinks and practicing good oral hygiene can extend your brighter smile.

Will whitening hurt or cause sensitivity? 02 //

Some patients experience temporary sensitivity during or after treatment. Our dentist monitors your comfort closely and can adjust the process to minimize any discomfort.

Does insurance cover teeth whitening? 03 //

Teeth whitening is considered a cosmetic procedure, so most insurance plans do not cover it. We offer flexible financing options and a membership plan to help make treatment affordable. Check our Insurance and Financing page for details.

How is professional whitening different from whitening strips? 04 //

Professional whitening uses stronger, dentist-grade gel that works faster and more evenly. Our in-office treatment delivers dramatic results in about an hour, while over-the-counter strips take weeks and may cause uneven whitening or irritation.
