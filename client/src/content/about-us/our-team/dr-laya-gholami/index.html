Specialized Practice

Specialty Areas & Clinical Focus

Periodontal Care & Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease affects more than just your smile: it has been linked to heart health, diabetes, and overall wellness. Dr. Gholami offers non-surgical and surgical periodontal treatment to stop the progression of gum disease and restore the health of your gums and supporting bone. Her goal is to help you keep your natural teeth for a lifetime. To learn more about gum health, visit our periodontics page.

01 //

Advanced Implant Surgery & Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

When one or more teeth are missing, dental implants are the closest replacement to natural teeth. Dr. Gholami plans and places implants with precision, using 3D imaging to ensure ideal positioning. She handles everything from a single tooth implant to full-mouth rehabilitation with implant supported bridges or dentures. For an overview of implant options, see our dental implants page.

02 //

Bone Augmentation & Grafting

Not everyone has enough jawbone to support implants. Dr. Gholami performs bone grafting to rebuild lost bone, creating a strong foundation for implant placement. Whether you need a small graft after a recent extraction or a larger sinus lift or ridge augmentation, she uses advanced techniques to make the process as comfortable as possible. Visit our bone grafting page for more detail.

Patient FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a periodontist, and when should I see one? 01 //

A periodontist is a dentist with three years of additional training beyond dental school that focuses on the gums and bone supporting your teeth. You should see a periodontist if you notice bleeding gums, gum recession, deep pockets between teeth and gums, loose teeth, or if you need dental implants or bone grafting. Dr. Gholami can evaluate your condition and recommend the most effective treatment.

Does Dr. Gholami accept new patients at your offices? 02 //

Yes, Dr. Gholami welcomes new patients at all four offices — Port St. Lucie, Saint Lucie West, Stuart, and Jupiter. Call us or use the contact form to schedule your first appointment. She works with patients of all ages and can answer any questions about gum health, implants, or what to expect during treatment.

What implant procedures does Dr. Gholami perform? 03 //

Dr. Gholami performs single and multiple dental implants, full-arch implant supported restorations like All-on-4, and bone grafting procedures. She uses digital planning tools to ensure each implant is placed precisely. Her goal is to restore function and appearance so you can eat, smile, and live confidently.
